Desert Founders Society — FICTIONAL CONSTITUTION
Article 1. Our mission is to help students build meaningful professional connections.
Article 2. The President approves organization plans and compliance decisions.
Article 3. The Programs Director plans member events and coordinates staffing.
Article 4. The Treasurer owns purchasing and funding applications.
Article 5. Marketing owns promotion and member communication.
Article 6. Outreach owns relationships with campus organizations.
Article 7. External Relations owns relationships with companies and sponsors.
Article 8. The Secretary maintains documentation and handoff notes.
