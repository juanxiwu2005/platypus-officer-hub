## 4. Over-reach, missing conditions, campus and version mixing, omissions

**Over-reaching or mis-weighted statements**
- "通常" for 3 credit hours: the source says "must" (R004 L357).
- The tabling-plus-vendor case and the end-of-semester funding case are written as "可能" or "分别判断". The sources give definite answers (R004 L590; R025 L4361, R038 L6576).
- Case 4 cites R014. It should cite R025 L4401 and R026 L4526–28.
- "Top 3 officer 的每项测验": the source says "a top 3 officer" (R037 L5429).
- R059 "排除 student organizations": the source covers ASU-owned, leased and rented vehicles only.
- The R024–038 grouping puts R036 under "Tempe funding". R036 applies university-wide.

**Campus and version mixing to fix**
- In R037's caps block, "$25 per expected guest" (L5936) sits under USG West, and "$850 / $700" figures appear under other campuses. Tempe is $850 (L5914). The Bylaws give $30 per person (R038 L6550).
- Travel: R027 and R038 both say $1,000 and 4 weeks. Per R028 and R038 L6491, the Bylaws outrank the handbook and the training ($500, 6 weeks). Teach the stricter timeline (6 weeks), and "confirm the cap with the Business Office".
- Food vetting: R036 (no FER at non-exclusive venues, zero priority violations) vs R041 (FER for all ready-to-eat food, "more than 1 violation"; slide dated "© 2021"). R015 L2424 says the "Approved Food Providers List is no longer available", but R037 L5709 still links it.
- Advisor-at-event triggers differ: R004 L453 (any external party), R011 L1760 (public or 250+), R041 L9125 (public, security risk, media, VIPs).
- The app's own `src/data/rules.ts` R13 ("No events during final exams") and its handbook source are the 2025–26 versions. R004 (2026–27) has changed this.

**Important omissions in the draft**
1. The chain Event Registry → RITM → Mazevo, with 14-day cancellation and the 16-day venue minimum (R014 L2294, R015 L2438).
2. Advertising before approval can cancel the event (R004 L598). The draft says "获批后宣传" but not the consequence.
3. Purchases made before approval are not reimbursed (R025 L4363, R038 L6625).
4. "Students do not have signature authority" (R037 L5676). Invoices must be billed to ASU (R029 L4891).
5. Event-funding caps: $15,000 per semester; presentations above $5k and $10k; printed marketing ≤ $500 per event; speakers ≤ $5,000 (R037 L5916–18, R038 L6552, L6556).
6. Speaker-deadline conflict: 14 business days vs 2 weeks vs 6 weeks (R029 L4857, R037 L5476/L5741, R041 L8452).
7. Business Meals Form signatures, receipts within 10 days, no rollover, 18% tip cap (R037 L5718, L5726, L5730, L5728).
8. Consult the Student Organizations Team before engaging vendors or doing sales (R004 L586).
9. Internal contradiction in the appeals clause: the 15 business-day bar vs the 10 business-day emergency (R038 L6638 vs L6648).
10. The Bylaws precedence clause (R038 L6491). The draft cites only R028's hierarchy.
