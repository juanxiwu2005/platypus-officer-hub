## Stage 1 delivery boundaries (historical; see Stage 3 for current state)

Stage 1 delivers working foundation/reducer, without claiming released email styling, Details, new status buttons, or real sending. Optional `reminderRuntime` uses versioned runtime; old v22 without it is preserved, without automatic resets, fake sends, guessed historical recipients, or duplicate old/new follow-ups. Only explicit `enableReminderPlan` initializes the new path. UI/unified activation comes in Stage 3.

Activation closes old open silenceCase records whose applicability cannot be proved, preserving existing leadership notices/send history. Preserve unknown/damaged runtime without executing it; do not reset archives to mask errors. At Stage 1, old composer may still call original AI; Stage 2 replaces single-reminder composition with rule email without waiting for AI. Ask/setup AI lies outside this change.

Candidates: start date, due date, third overdue day. On coincidence choose one more relevant email; late opening selects only the most relevant current candidate, without catch-up bursts. At most three per task/owner term/recipient, six per recipient per rolling seven calendar days. Independent helper follow-ups do not consume the original owner's allowance, but count against the helper's. At most one per task/recipient/day. Rescheduling does not reset same-term limits. Actual demo send date is operation day, never backdated to plan. Preserve custom settings; Stage 3 migration must be visible, never silently overwrite them.

Statuses: Working on it stops not-started reminders but retains due checks; Waiting on pauses ordinary owner reminders and creates follow-up for explicit helpers; Stuck pauses ordinary reminders and retains help notices; Done stops candidates while preserving history. Rescheduling recalculates future candidates; reassignment assesses a new term, preserving historical recipients. When new mode is active, old 3/5-day member follow-ups do not also run; leadership follow-up retains permissions and closes/rebuilds cases on completion/reply/rescheduling.
