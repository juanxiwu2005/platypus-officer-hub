# SEO workflow reference: sources for playbooks

Platypus v1.0's default playbooks, silence protocol, and onboarding checklist come from the ASU student entrepreneurship organization SEO's own operating documents. This is a summary; originals remain in Juanxi's local `05_Phase3_Prototype/Reference/` folder and are not committed. No member names or contact details are included.

## OA101 event initiation (lanes: president, outreach, programs, marketing, finance, EVP)

| Step | Owner | Work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00 Start · Opportunity intake | President | Route external opportunities to the president first to check mission, priority, and available capacity; decline poor fits early. |
| 10 Contact partner | Outreach (VP External / Outreach) | Arrange the first partner meeting with Programs; clarify both sides' goals, audience, format, and value for members. |
| 20 Draft event plan | Programs (Professional Development / Programs) | Estimate attendance, venue, staffing/volunteers, food, and initial publicity direction. |
| 30 Marketing review and estimate | Marketing | Identify flyers, online content, tabling, giveaways, and preliminary costs. |
| 40 Budget review | Finance | Estimate venue/staffing/food/publicity costs, identify funding sources, and remove nonessential items. |
| 50 Plan approval | President | Check mission fit, feasibility, and strategic value; return for revision if needed. |
| 60 Execute | Everyone | Follow OA102. |
| 70 Event feedback | EVP | Collect feedback within 3 days after the event and write a short review. |
| 80 End · Archive | EVP | Archive the plan, publicity materials, attendance, budget records, partner notes, feedback, and lessons. |

## OA102 execution

61 Programs issues the event plan → 62 Marketing prepares materials / 63 Finance prepares the budget / 64 EVP prepares logistics (venue, equipment, Special Event Registry, Sun Devil Central event) → 65 Outreach synchronizes information and obtains the partner's review of publicity → 66 Budget ready / 67 Materials published / 68 Logistics ready → 69 Event starts.

## OA201 funding (Finance)

Create budget list → Over budget? (Yes → cut and recalculate) → Apply for funding and complete the application → Create purchase order → Receive goods/services → Finalize budget list.

The strict College-funding sequence (Juanxi, L5): budget request → budget approval → purchase request → purchase approval → order or reimbursement → submit attendance list. Track the current stage and whether funds have been used, rather than building a general ledger.

## Event-planning timing checklist (T-90 / T-60 / T-30 / T-14)

- **T-90:** first partner meeting (who we are, goals, mutual benefit, draft topic); catering plan; funding plan for food/publicity/parking/etc.; Special Event Registry; Sun Devil Central event registration; venue reservation.
- **T-60:** audience research; publicity materials/design; host; Plan B.
- **T-30:** purchase orders for food/publicity/etc.; slide review; second partner meeting; staffing; parking-permit requests.
- **T-14:** execute the publicity plan; full review of venue/food/publicity/logistics/staffing; quality gate for speakers/partners.

Other template sections: value-exchange map (what we have / what they need / what we need / what they have), meeting notes, minute-by-minute event schedule, budget list, publicity plan (Levels 1–3, channels/deadlines), venue candidates, and staffing for check-in/hosting/photography/member interaction.

## Ghost Owner Protocol (source of Platypus silence-protocol defaults)

Trigger: the owner has provided no update for 7 consecutive days or missed two critical milestones. Light reminders on days 3/5; the president sets a 24-hour response deadline on day 7; designate and record a temporary owner on day 8; that owner publishes a new timeline on day 10 and conducts a review within 7 days. Keep the entire process private.

## Risk register summary

Missing owner, highest impact; delayed funding, the most common external constraint, managed as a first-class dependency with early Plan B if approval misses an agreed checkpoint; too few registrations, monitored through registration pace with early cancellation/rescheduling if necessary; excessive scope, addressed by pausing low-impact work; leadership transition, requiring every project to update SOPs, partner records, and its review within 7 days.

## Incoming-officer checklist (source of Handoff's first-week checklist)

0. Confirmation within 3 days: sign the offer, supply contact details, join the group, assign a buddy.
1. Align mission/goals/expectations within 7 days: explain the mission; record personal needs/interests and 1–3 semester outcomes; establish rule boundaries, resources, evaluation/reporting cadence, and consequences; sign a one-page delegation agreement.
2. Six leadership-course modules within 1 month.
3. Technology/systems within 7 days: organization email, calendar sync, internal systems, venue-booking/event-registration ability, ASU funding policy, and file-storage rules.
4. Follow-up: week-2 check and day-30 1:1 review.

## Team-charter semester-goal examples (suggestions for setup step 2)
